Appointment Reminder Flow

No-shows are a silent killer for franchise businesses. Every missed appointment is wasted time, wasted ad spend, and a missed membership sale. We build multi-touch reminder sequences that fire at exactly the right moments: 24 hours before, 1 hour before, and 5 minutes before. Each reminder gives them a chance to confirm, reschedule, or cancel. The result: no-show rates drop by 30-40% within the first month.

  • 24-hour SMS + email reminder with confirm / reschedule / cancel options
  • 1-hour SMS reminder for the final push before the appointment
  • 5-minute SMS with directions, parking info, and what to bring

We fire an automated recovery sequence within 15 minutes of a missed appointment: empathetic tone, easy rebooking link, no pressure. About 35-45% of no-shows end up rebooking when the follow-up is fast and low-friction, which means every franchisee gets a second shot at conversion without any extra work.

  • 15-minute recovery trigger that fires the moment an appointment is marked as missed
